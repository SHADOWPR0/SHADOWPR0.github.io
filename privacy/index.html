Privacy notice

This notice covers a personal Query Link OAuth configuration and this public information site.

Operator and purpose

The account owner operates this personal integration to connect their Google account to Grok's Google Cloud BigQuery connector. It is used for user-directed dataset exploration, queries, analysis, and related authorized BigQuery operations. It is not offered for public signup.

Information accessed

The configured authorization requests OpenID identity, an email address, and the Google BigQuery scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/bigquery. Depending on the request and the account's existing Google Cloud permissions, access can include project, dataset and table metadata, SQL statements, job metadata, query results, and table contents. The BigQuery scope permits management operations as well as reading. It does not override Google Cloud IAM.

Use and sharing

Google processes account authentication, consent, and BigQuery operations. Grok's connector receives OAuth authorization and the information returned by requested tools, which may appear in Grok conversations and responses. These transfers are needed to carry out the connected user's requests. The owner does not operate a separate intermediary data service for this connection.

The owner does not sell Google user data obtained through this integration, use it for advertising, or intentionally transfer it for unrelated purposes. The integration's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Google and Grok's provider independently operate their services. Their processing, retention, security, and available data controls are described in the Google Privacy Policy, Google Cloud Privacy Notice, and Grok provider privacy policy. This notice does not promise that the provider never retains or uses conversation content; the applicable product terms and account settings control that processing.

Storage and retention

Source datasets remain in Google Cloud unless an authorized request exports or copies them. Google may retain job and audit records. Grok may retain connection credentials, conversation content, tool results, and related records under its policies and account controls. Any results the owner chooses to save are retained in his chosen destination until he removes them, subject to that destination's retention rules.

This public site stores no OAuth credentials, SQL results, or account data in an application database. Its source contains only public app documentation and site-verification information. There are no analytics scripts or contact forms. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may log IP addresses and other request information for security under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Control, revocation, and deletion

Disconnect the Google Cloud BigQuery connector in Grok to stop using that connection. Revoke its Google account authorization through Google Account third-party connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete information already returned or saved.

Use Grok's account and conversation controls for provider-held history, and Google Cloud controls for datasets, jobs, and retention settings. To request removal of copies under the operator's control or ask a privacy question, contact the site's GitHub issues page. Provider-held records follow the applicable provider's deletion and retention procedures.

Updates

This notice will be updated if the integration's purpose or handling practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
